Why it is here rather than on one page: Frontier's media usage rules ask that attribution sit somewhere "easily located by others", naming the header, footer or terms for a community site, and they prefer the long form where it fits — a footer fits. Before this, the long form was on index.md and nowhere else, so a reader landing straight on a capability page built out of Frontier's data saw none of it. The wording is site.frontier_attribution in _config.yml, so the site states it once and this file and index.md cannot drift apart.
